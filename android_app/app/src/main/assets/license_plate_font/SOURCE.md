# License-plate glyph assets

The `140_*.jpg` files are the 140 mm single-line plate glyph templates from
[`Pengfei8324/chinese_license_plate_generator`](https://github.com/Pengfei8324/chinese_license_plate_generator),
used under its MIT license (Copyright 2020 leijd).

They are an open-source visual reconstruction of Chinese number-plate glyphs,
not the nationwide anti-counterfeit production dies specified by GA 36-2018.
This project uses them only for an on-screen simulation. Physical security
features, maker marks, reflective film and production identifiers are outside
the scope of the generator.

For the supported 440 mm x 140 mm small conventional-car plate, the app draws
each template into the 45 mm x 90 mm slots specified in Figure 1 of GA 36-2018.
